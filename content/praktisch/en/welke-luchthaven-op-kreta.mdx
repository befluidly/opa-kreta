\*When you travel to Crete, your vacation actually starts in the air. You see the island appear 
below you — a mix of sea, mountains and small villages — and you feel your trip is almost 
beginning. But one choice can shape your experience: which airport do you land at?\*

\*Crete has three airports: **Heraklion**, **Chania** and **Sitia**. In practice, nearly all 
travelers fly into the first two. Below is a calm, clear overview of what each airport means for 
your vacation.\*

## Heraklion (HER) – Central and Ideal If You Want to See a Lot

Heraklion is Crete's largest airport. It can be a bit busier at times, but you get through quickly 
and land right in the center of the island. To me, the 
[region around Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion) feels the most versatile. You'll find 
[Knossos](/gidsen/heraklion/knossos), the [Archaeological Museum](/gidsen/heraklion/musea-van-heraklion), 
lively cities, but also smaller mountain villages, coastal spots and plenty of historical and 
cultural sites.

Those who want to explore different sides of the island are simply well-positioned starting from 
[Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion).

## Chania (CHQ) – Quieter, Nature, Beaches and Impressive Gorges

Chania's airport lies in [western Crete](/gidsen/chania) and feels smaller and more relaxed. The 
region is known for its nature and beautiful [beaches](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-stranden) 
such as **Falassarna**, **Elafonissi** and **Balos**, and for the well-known 
[gorges](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven) **Samaria**, **Imbros** and **Aradena**.

There's certainly culture to be found, but to me, [Chania](/gidsen/chania/musea-van-chania) mainly 
feels like a place for peace, nature and hiking. For me, Chania is more for relaxation and nature, 
Heraklion more for exploring.

## Sitia (JSH) – Charming Town, But Not Really Relevant as an Airport

Sitia does have an airport, but international flights are rare there. For most travelers, it's 
simply not a realistic option. It's mainly used for domestic traffic.

## Which Airport Should You Choose?

### Choose Heraklion If You:

- want to see and do a lot
- find a central starting point convenient
- are looking for culture and variety
- want to explore the whole island

### Choose Chania If You:

- are mainly coming for [nature and beaches](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-stranden)
- enjoy [hiking](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven)
- want a quieter start and end to your trip
- are planning your vacation in the west

### Sitia:

- only suitable if you're flying via Athens or find an exceptional connection

## One More Practical Consideration

Chania sometimes looks cheaper, but take a moment to consider where you're staying. Crete is 
large, and an extra hour of driving can really make a difference — especially on arrival day.

## In Closing

Whichever airport you choose, the moment the plane descends through the clouds and the island 
comes into view remains special. Crete welcomes you with softer light and a calm atmosphere you 
feel right away. Just choose the airport that fits your plans. The rest follows naturally, at the 
island's own slow and warm rhythm.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Airports on Crete

### Which Airport on Crete Is Best?

That depends on where you're staying. If you're staying in the east or central part of the island 
(such as Chersonissos, Malia or Agios Nikolaos), Heraklion is usually the best choice. If you're 
staying in the west (Chania, Kissamos or Elafonissi), Chania is more practical.

### Is Heraklion or Chania Cheaper?

That depends heavily on the season, your departure airport and the type of flight. In summer, 
there are usually more charter and direct flights to Heraklion, which can sometimes push prices 
slightly lower due to greater competition. In spring and fall, Chania can turn out to be more 
affordable, depending on the airline.

It's always worth comparing both airports, especially if you're flexible with your travel dates. 
Sometimes the difference in ticket price is small, but the extra travel time on Crete can offset 
that price advantage.

### How Far Apart Are the Airports?

The distance between Heraklion (HER) and Chania (CHQ) is about 145 kilometers. By car, that 
typically takes 2 to 2.5 hours, depending on traffic and the season.

The route runs mostly along the national road on Crete's north coast. Anyone staying in 
[Rethymnon](/gidsen/rethymnon/bezoek-rethymno), for example, is roughly halfway between the two 
airports.

### Is Sitia an Option for International Flights?

Sitia Airport (JSH) is used mainly for domestic flights, especially from Athens. International 
connections are rare and usually seasonal.

For most travelers, Sitia is therefore not a realistic option, unless you're deliberately staying 
in the far east of Crete and traveling via Athens.

### Which Airport on Crete Is Closest to My Hotel?

That depends entirely on the region where you're staying. If you're staying in the east or central 
Crete — such as Chersonissos, Malia, Agios Nikolaos or Elounda — Heraklion (HER) is almost always 
the closest airport.

If you're staying in the west — such as Chania, Kissamos, Falassarna or Elafonissi — Chania (CHQ) 
is usually the better choice.

For those staying in [Rethymnon](/gidsen/rethymnon/bezoek-rethymno), both airports are relatively 
convenient. Rethymnon lies roughly halfway between Heraklion and Chania, so flight price can 
sometimes be the deciding factor.
