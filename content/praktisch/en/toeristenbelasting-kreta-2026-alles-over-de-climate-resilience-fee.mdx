\\*Many travelers only find out at checkout: an extra charge on the bill that was never clearly 

mentioned beforehand. It's not a mistake by the hotel — it's a mandatory Greek fee that's been 

around for two years now, but still causes plenty of confusion. Here's exactly what it is, how 

much you'll pay in Crete, and how to avoid being caught by surprise.\\*

## What Exactly Is the Climate Resilience Fee?

Its full name is the \*\*Climate Crisis Resilience Fee\*\*. It's a nightly charge that replaced 

Greece's old accommodation tax as of January 1, 2024. On January 1, 2025, the rates increased 

significantly, and the high season shifted from March to April. These rates remain unchanged for 

2026.

The fee was introduced following the severe wildfires and floods that hit Greece in recent years, 

and it funds a reserve for disaster response and climate adaptation.

## Why Does It Feel Like a Surprise?

Two reasons:

1. \*\*It's rarely shown at booking time.\*\* The fee is collected separately by the accommodation 

itself, usually at check-in or check-out — not through the booking platform where you originally 

reserved.

2. \*\*It's per room, not per person.\*\* A couple in one room pays the same as a family of four in 

that same room — logical once you know it, but not immediately intuitive.

## How Much Will You Pay in Crete in 2026?

The rates are national and identical on every Greek island, including Crete. They depend on the 

type of accommodation, its official star rating, and the season.

### High Season (April through October)

| Accommodation | Amount per night |

| 1- and 2-star hotel | €2.00 |

| 3-star hotel | €5.00 |

| 4-star hotel | €10.00 |

| 5-star hotel | €15.00 |

| Rooms/apartments for rent | €2.00 |

| Short-term rental (e.g. Airbnb, <80m²) | €8.00 |

| Detached house/villa (>80m²) | €15.00 |

### Low Season (November through March)

| Accommodation | Amount per night |

| 1- and 2-star hotel | €0.50 |

| 3-star hotel | €1.50 |

| 4-star hotel | €3.00 |

| 5-star hotel | €4.00 |

| Rooms/apartments for rent | €0.50 |

| Short-term rental (e.g. Airbnb, <80m²) | €2.00 |

| Detached house/villa (>80m²) | €4.00 |

## A Concrete Example

Booking a 4-star hotel in Crete for 7 nights in July? Expect an extra €70 on top of your room 

rate (€10 x 7 nights), per room — regardless of whether 1 or 4 people are staying in that room.

Traveling to the same hotel in November? You'd pay just €21 for those same 7 nights (€3 x 7).

## How and When Do You Pay It?

- The fee is \*\*not paid online in advance\*\* — you pay it locally, directly to the accommodation, 

usually at checkout.

- You'll receive a separate receipt for this payment.
- Payment is usually in cash or by card, depending on the accommodation — it's never a bad idea 

to keep some cash on hand.

- This fee is not included in the price shown on Booking.com, Airbnb, or other platforms — always 

check the fine print or the "price breakdown" of your booking.

## Does This Apply to Everyone?

Yes, the fee applies to nearly every form of paid tourist accommodation across all of Greece — so 

everywhere in Crete, from Chania to Sitia. Exceptions exist for certain categories such as youth 

hostels and campsites.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this the same as the old accommodation tax?

No, it fully replaced that as of 2024, with higher amounts and a different structure (now tied to 

official star ratings instead of a flat per-night tax).

### Do I pay this per person or per room?

Per room/unit per night — not per person.

### Can I pay this in advance when booking?

In most cases, no. The fee is collected separately by the accommodation itself, independent of 

your booking platform.

### Will this amount change again in 2026?

Not at the moment — the rates set in January 2025 remain unchanged throughout 2026.

## In Short

It's not a hidden trick or a hotel's mistake — just a fixed, relatively modest Greek fee you're 

now prepared for. Just budget a few extra euros per night into your 

[travel budget](/praktisch/beste-tijd-kreta), and you won't be caught off guard at checkout.
