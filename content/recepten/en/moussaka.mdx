## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 2 medium eggplants
- 2 potatoes
- 400 g ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed
- 400 ml chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 50 g Parmesan cheese (grated)
- Pepper and salt

For the béchamel sauce:
- 50 g butter
- 50 g flour
- 400 ml milk
- 1 egg yolk
- A pinch of nutmeg

## Preparation

1. **Prepare the vegetables**
   Slice the eggplants, sprinkle with salt and let drain for 15 minutes.
   Rinse them afterward, pat dry and fry briefly in olive oil.
   Also cut the potatoes into thin slices and fry until golden brown.
2. **Make the meat sauce**
   Heat some olive oil in a pan. Sauté the onion and garlic until translucent.
   Add the ground beef and cook until crumbly. Stir in the tomato paste, cinnamon and oregano.
   Add the chopped tomatoes and let the sauce simmer gently for 15 minutes. Season with pepper and salt.
3. **Béchamel sauce**
   Melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and let cook for 2 minutes.
   Gradually stir in the milk until you have a smooth sauce. Season with nutmeg, pepper and salt.
   Stir in the egg yolk off the heat.
4. **Assemble for the oven**
   Grease an oven dish with olive oil. Layer first a layer of potato, then eggplant, then the meat sauce.
   Repeat and finish with béchamel. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.
5. **Bake**
   Bake for 35–40 minutes at 180°C until the top is golden brown.
   Let it rest briefly before serving — the flavors get even better!

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Delicious with a fresh salad and a glass of white wine.
- Moussaka even tastes better the next day — perfect for reheating.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 3 days.
