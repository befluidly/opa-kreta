## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 700 g fresh green (snap) beans, trimmed
- 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 120 ml olive oil
- 1 can (400 g) chopped peeled tomatoes, or a good-quality tomato sauce
- 500 ml water
- 3 potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Feta and fresh bread, to serve

## Preparation

1. **Prepare the beans**
   Trim the green beans (snip off both ends) and rinse them well. Set aside.
2. **Sauté the onion and garlic**
   Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Sauté the onion and garlic, 
   stirring well so they don't burn. Once the onion starts turning translucent, add the 
   green beans and stir well.
3. **Add the rest**
   Add the tomatoes (or tomato sauce), the water, the potato chunks, the salt and the 
   pepper. Stir everything together well.
4. **Let it simmer**
   Cover the pan and let it simmer for 50 to 60 minutes over medium-high heat. Give the 
   pan a gentle shake every so often (about every 15 minutes), and check occasionally 
   that there's still enough liquid in the pan. If not, add a splash of extra water as 
   needed.
5. **Let it rest**
   Remove the pan from the heat and let it rest, uncovered, for 30 minutes before 
   serving. This lets the flavors settle in even further.

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Serve with a generous chunk of feta, some olives, and definitely fresh bread — that 
  bread is essential for soaking up the olive oil and tomato sauce.
- No fresh beans on hand? Frozen green beans work well too — no need to thaw, just add 
  them straight to the pan. Expect a slightly longer cooking time, and use a bit less 
  water, since frozen beans release moisture of their own as they cook.
- Fasolakia often tastes even better the next day, once all the flavors have had time 
  to settle.
- Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge.
