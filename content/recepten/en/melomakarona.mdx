## Ingredients

For ±40 cookies:

**For the dough**

- 125 ml olive oil
- 125 ml vegetable oil
- 100 g fine sugar
- 100 g fresh orange juice
- 90 g water
- 4 tablespoons cognac (I use Metaxa)
- 50 g honey
- Grated orange zest of 2 oranges
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1/3 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 500 g flour
- 150 g semolina
- 1/2 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 tablespoon baking soda

**For the honey syrup**

- 300 g water
- 200 g honey
- 600 g sugar
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 3 cloves
- 1 orange, cut into 4 pieces

**For the finishing touch**

- 100 g finely chopped walnuts
- A little ground cinnamon

## Preparation

1. **Make the honey syrup**
Put all the syrup ingredients into a pot and let the syrup boil for 5 minutes.
Once all the sugar has dissolved, take the pot off the heat so the syrup can cool
while you make the cookies.

2. **Make the dough – liquid mixture**
In a large bowl, mix the olive oil, vegetable oil, honey, cognac, sugar, water, orange juice, vanilla extract,
orange zest and the spices. Add the baking powder and baking soda to this mixture and stir
well until the sugar has dissolved and the mixture starts to foam.

2. **Make the dough – flour mixture**
In a second bowl, mix the flour, semolina, baking powder and baking soda. Stir well!
Then gradually add the liquid mixture to the flour mixture
and knead until you have a slightly oily dough that doesn't stick.

2. **Shape the cookies**
Preheat the oven to 180°C. Take pieces of dough about the size of a walnut and roll them into oval-shaped cookies.
Place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and press each oval cookie lightly flat with a fork. Then
prick three holes in each cookie with a toothpick (halfway through — not all the way), so the baked
cookies can absorb the syrup properly later.

3. **Bake**
Bake the melomakarona for 15 to 20 minutes, until golden brown. Let them cool briefly.

4. **Dip the cookies**
As soon as they come out of the oven, dip them immediately in the syrup for 20 seconds. Make sure they're
fully submerged. Then place them on a plate and sprinkle with chopped walnuts and a touch of cinnamon.

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Melomakarona taste best after resting for a day, so the honey has time to fully settle into the cookie.
- They keep well for weeks in a sealed tin — perfect for sharing during the holidays.
- Serve with Greek coffee or a glass of sweet dessert wine.
- And don't forget: **Team Melomakarona** wins every year 😉
