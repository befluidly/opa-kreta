## Ingredients

For 6 to 8 servings:

- 500 g fresh spinach (or 400 g frozen, well drained)
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 1 spring onion, sliced into rings
- 2 cloves garlic (finely chopped or pureed)
- 2 to 3 tbsp olive oil
- 200 g feta, crumbled
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped (I usually use dill from the freezer)
- A pinch of nutmeg
- Pepper to taste (salt is rarely needed because of the feta)
- 8 sheets of filo pastry (I use fresh filo from the supermarket fridge)
- 100 g melted butter or olive oil for brushing the sheets
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Preparation

***Pie Dish Version***

1. **Make the filling**
Heat the olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic, onion and spring onion until translucent. Add the spinach
(in batches if there's a lot) and let it wilt until all the moisture has evaporated (if using spinach from
the freezer, you can skip this step and just mix the onion mixture straight in). Let it cool briefly.
In a bowl, mix the spinach with the crumbled feta, eggs, dill, nutmeg and a bit of pepper.

2. **Prepare the pastry**
Preheat the oven to 180°C. Lightly grease an oven dish (about 30 × 20 cm) with butter or oil.
Place a sheet of filo in the dish, brush it with butter or oil, and repeat with four sheets —
make sure the edges overhang slightly.

3. **Fill and cover**
Spread the spinach filling evenly over the base. Cover with the remaining four filo sheets,
each brushed with butter or oil. Fold the overhanging edges inward and brush the top once more.

4. **Bake**
Carefully cut the top layer of pastry into diamonds or rectangles (this prevents it from bursting open while baking).
Bake the spanakopita for 40 to 45 minutes, until golden brown and crispy.

***Strifti (Rolled Version)***

The rolled version is almost identical. Lightly grease your countertop with a bit of olive oil and place the first sheet of filo
on top. Brush it with oil, place a second sheet on top and brush again. A few centimeters from the edge, spread a thin
strip of the spinach mixture along the length of the sheets (about 3 cm wide). Fold the edge closest to the
mixture over it and roll to the end of the sheet — as tightly as possible.
Place the roll in a round baking dish: start at the outer edge and work inward to form a spiral.
Bake at 180°C until golden brown and crispy (about 45 minutes).

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Let it cool briefly — it tastes best lukewarm or at room temperature.
- Serve with a piece of feta, some olives and a glass of retsina or white wine.
- Greeks and Cretans also eat this as a savory breakfast with a cup of coffee.
- Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge, or can be frozen per piece.
