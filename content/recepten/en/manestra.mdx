## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 250 g kritharaki (orzo)
- 450 g ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 170 g tomato paste
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon or a small piece of cinnamon stick
- 750 ml to 1 liter water or stock
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Optionally, some grated cheese (kefalotyri or Parmesan) to serve

## Preparation

1. **Brown the meat**
Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat in a large, heavy pot with a lid (such as a Dutch oven).
Add the finely chopped onion and garlic and cook until softened and lightly browned,
about 3 minutes. Add the ground beef (450 g) and cook until fully browned, breaking it
up with a wooden spoon, about 5 minutes.
2. **Make the sauce**
Add the tomato paste (170 g), 1 teaspoon salt, black pepper to taste and
1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon. Stir well so the meat is coated with the tomato paste and
seasoning, and cook until the tomato paste starts to darken slightly, about 2 minutes.
3. **Add the orzo**
Pour in the water or stock and bring to a boil. Stir in the orzo,
lower the heat and let simmer for 20 minutes, until the pasta is cooked and the sauce turns syrupy.
Stir regularly. Add a little extra hot water if needed to reach the desired thickness.
4. **Taste and serve**
Remove the bay leaf, taste and adjust seasoning. Serve the manestra warm,
sprinkled with a little grated cheese or a drizzle of olive oil.

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Manestra tastes best when it's just slightly thickened — creamy, not dry.
- Serve with a fresh salad or some feta and crusty bread.
- The next day it's even better: the cinnamon and tomato will have fully soaked into the pasta.
- Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge or 1 month in the freezer, perfect as a quick warm lunch.
