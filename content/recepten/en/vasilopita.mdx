## Equipment Needed

- Round baking pan, 25 cm diameter
- Food processor or hand mixer

## Ingredients

- 420 g sifted flour (all-purpose flour)
- 2 teaspoons baking powder (± 8 g)
- 1 pinch of salt
- 225 g unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 400 g fine granulated sugar
- 6 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon orange zest, finely grated and well packed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons cognac or brandy (preferably Metaxa)
- 240 ml milk
- 40 g powdered sugar (for dusting the top)

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (top and bottom heat).
Place an oven rack in the middle of the oven.

2. Put the sifted flour, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
Mix everything with a whisk or fork and set aside.

3. In the bowl of a food processor (or with a hand mixer), beat the butter and sugar together.
Start slowly and don't go above medium speed.
Beat for 4–5 minutes, until the mixture is light, creamy and smooth.

4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each one.
Then add the orange zest, vanilla extract and cognac.
Mix until everything is well incorporated.

5. Now add about a third of the flour and mix on low speed.
Then pour in the milk, followed by the rest of the flour.
Keep mixing on low speed to avoid splattering.
Mix for another 1 to 2 minutes, until you have a smooth batter with no lumps.

6. Lightly grease a round 25 cm baking pan with vegetable oil.
Line the bottom with parchment paper.

7. Take a clean silver coin (for example a 1- or 2-euro coin), wash and dry it well.
Wrap the coin tightly in food-safe plastic wrap or aluminum foil.

8. Pour the batter into the baking pan and smooth the top.
Make a small indentation in the batter with a knife and carefully slide the coin in.
Make sure the coin isn't visible on top.
Smooth the batter over again.

9. Bake the vasilopita for 60–70 minutes in the middle of the oven.
Starting at 55 minutes, test with a skewer:
if it comes out clean (a few crumbs are fine, but no wet batter), the cake is done.

10. Let the cake cool in the pan for 5 minutes.
Then carefully remove it from the pan and let it cool completely on a rack.

11. Just before serving, dust the top with powdered sugar.

## NOTE!

Be careful when serving to young children and the elderly:
the coin can pose a choking hazard.

Enjoy — and Καλή Χρονιά! (Happy New Year)
