## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 500 g fresh spinach (or 400 g frozen, well drained)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 spring onions, sliced into rings
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 150 g rice (preferably medium-grain, such as risotto rice or Carolina rice)
- 250 ml water or vegetable stock
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Optional: feta, to serve alongside

## Preparation

1. **Sauté the base**
Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onion and spring onion and let them sauté gently until translucent and fragrant.

2. **Add the spinach**
Add the spinach a bit at a time until it has all wilted. Stir well so most of the moisture evaporates.

3. **Cook the rice**
Add the rice and stir briefly so the grains absorb the flavor.
Pour in the water or stock, season with salt and pepper, and let it cook over low heat until the rice is tender and the mixture is creamy — not dry, not too wet.

4. **Finishing touches**
Stir in the dill and lemon juice. Taste and add a little extra olive oil or lemon if you'd like more freshness.

## Serving & Storage Tips

- Spanakorizo tastes best lukewarm or at room temperature, with a drizzle of good olive oil and some crumbled feta on top.
- Serve, if you like, with a piece of fresh bread or a Greek salad on the side.
- Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge; it's also delicious cold as a light lunch.
