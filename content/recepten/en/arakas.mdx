## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 800 g fresh green peas or 500 g frozen peas
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1–2 potatoes, cut into small cubes (optional)
- 3–4 medium carrots, peeled and sliced
- 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- 60 g tomato paste
- 2 cups water
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- ½ cup parsley, finely chopped
- ¼ cup dill, finely chopped
- Feta, for crumbling over the dish when serving
- Extra virgin olive oil, for an extra drizzle when serving

## Preparation

1. \*\*Sauté the vegetables and garlic\*\*

Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion until soft and translucent.

Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant. Add the peas

(no need to thaw frozen ones) and stir for a few minutes. Add the sliced carrots

and, if using, the diced potatoes.

2. \*\*Make the sauce\*\*

Add the can of chopped tomatoes and the 60 g of tomato paste. Mix everything well.

Pour in the water, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.

3. \*\*Add the herbs\*\*

Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Then add the parsley and

dill and stir well again.

4. \*\*Let it simmer\*\*

Let the dish simmer gently for about 40–45 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and

the sauce has thickened slightly. Stir occasionally and add a little extra water if it becomes too dry.

## Serving & Storage Tips

Serve warm, crumble some feta on top and finish with a generous drizzle of good

olive oil. Serve with fresh bread to soak up that rich sauce — that's how it's done.
