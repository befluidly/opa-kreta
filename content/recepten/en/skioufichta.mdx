## Ingredients

For 4 people:

- 400 g skioufichta (or another short, hollow pasta such as strozzapreti or orecchiette as an alternative)
- 4 to 5 tbsp good extra virgin olive oil
- 150 g grated mizithra (or Parmesan, Pecorino Romano if mizithra isn't available)
- Black pepper, freshly ground
- Optionally, a small knob of butter or a spoonful of pasta water for extra creaminess

## Preparation

1. Cook the pasta
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and
cook the skioufichta until just al dente, following the instructions on the
packaging. Reserve a cup of the cooking water for later.

2. Warm the oil
While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large pan over
low heat. It should become warm, but not smoke — this way it keeps its fruity flavor.

3. Combine everything
Drain the pasta and add it straight to the pan with the olive oil. Stir well,
add a splash of the cooking water and mix until the pasta is glossy and everything is evenly coated.

4. Add the cheese
Sprinkle the grated mizithra over the pasta and keep stirring gently until
it starts to melt slightly and blends with the oil into a creamy coating.
Season generously with freshly ground black pepper.

## Serving & Storage Tips
- Serve the skioufichta warm, with a bit of extra mizithra and a drizzle of raw olive oil on top.
- In some villages, people add a few fried pieces of apaki (smoked pork) or
a few cherry tomatoes, but it stays most traditional when kept pure.
