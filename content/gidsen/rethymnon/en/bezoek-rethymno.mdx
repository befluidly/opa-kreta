## Why Rethymno Is a Must on Crete

Rethymno lies between Chania and Heraklion and is often considered the most charming city on the 
island.

What makes Rethymno so special:

- a beautiful Venetian old town
- long sandy beaches right next to the center
- a cozy atmosphere with plenty of restaurants and terraces

It's less busy than Chania, but livelier than many smaller villages.

### A Brief History of Rethymno

Rethymno rose to prominence during the Venetian period (13th–17th century), when the harbor 
served as a stopover between Chania and [Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion). The 
Venetians gave the city its distinctive architecture: elegant loggias, fountains and mansions with 
carved portals.

After 1646, Rethymno came under Ottoman rule, which is still visible in the minarets and 
courtyards with mosaics. That blend of Venetian and Turkish influences still shapes the character 
of the old town today.

## What to Do in Rethymno Town

The old town is without doubt the highlight.

Here you'll find:

- narrow streets
- Venetian buildings
- hidden little squares
- charming shops and taverns

👉 Tip: just wander here without a plan — that's the best part.

### Rimondi Fountain

In the middle of the old town lies the well-known Rimondi Fountain from the 17th century. A 
small, but iconic spot you're bound to pass by anyway.

### Fortezza of Rethymno

Above the city sits the impressive Fortezza, an old Venetian fortress.

Why you should go:

- stunning sea views
- a lovely walk along the walls
- interesting history

👉 Best time: morning or sunset (cooler + better light)

### Rethymno Beach

Rethymno has one of the longest beaches on Crete. The beach begins right by the city and stretches 
on for kilometers.

Here you'll find:

- beach bars and sunbeds
- quieter spots outside the center
- water sports options

Perfect if you want to combine city and beach.

### Venetian Harbor

The old harbor is small but full of charm.

Here you can:

- enjoy the sunset
- eat well right by the water
- stroll among the boats

In the evening, this spot really comes alive.

### Eating and Drinking in Rethymno

Rethymno is a top destination for great food.

Be sure to try:

- fresh fish
- Greek mezze
- local wine or raki

You'll find the best restaurants in the old town.

### Shopping in Rethymno

- Nice things to buy:
- olive oil and herbs
- local products
- souvenirs

Perfect for bringing a piece of Crete back home.

## Combining Rethymno with Other Places

Rethymno makes an ideal base.

### Historical and Cultural Trips from Rethymno

- **Arkadi Monastery:** a 25-minute drive away. This 
[monastery](/gidsen/rethymnon/van-rethymno-tot-triopetra) played an important role during the 
Cretan uprising against the Ottomans. The atmosphere is subdued, and the view over the valley is 
impressive.
- **Amari Valley:** a green, fertile region with [olive groves](/gidsen/eten-en-drinken/bezoek-olijfboeren) 
and villages like Thronos and Meronas. Ideal for a relaxed drive with stops at local taverns.
- **Preveli Beach:** on the south coast, known for its palm trees and river mouth. Combine it with 
a visit to Preveli Monastery, perched high above the coast.

### Hiking the Gorges around Rethymno

The Rethymno region has several [gorges](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven) that are 
less busy than the famous Samaria Gorge, but just as beautiful.

- **Mili Gorge:**
Just 5 km from Rethymno. An easy hike (about 1.5 hours round trip) through a green gorge with 
waterfalls, abandoned mills and the ruins of an old village. Well-suited for families.
- **Kourtaliotiko Gorge:**
Spectacular and rugged, with steep rock walls and a river that partly dries up in summer. The 
gorge lies on the route to Preveli and is reached via stairs from the main road. You'll hear the 
sound of flowing water before you see it.
- **Prassano Gorge:**
Close to Rethymno, heading east toward the village of Prasses. A quiet gorge with narrow passages, 
ideal in spring when there's still water flowing. Suited to hikers with some experience.
- **Patsos Gorge (Agios Antonios):**
Located inland, toward the Amari Valley. Short but impressive, with wooden footbridges, a small 
cave church and plenty of shade. Perfect for half a day.

**_Tip:_** Always bring good hiking shoes, water and sun protection. Most 
[gorges](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven) have no facilities, so set out early in the 
day and check accessibility in advance during the summer months.
