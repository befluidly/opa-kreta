Looking for the most beautiful attractions in East Crete? In this complete guide you'll discover 
what to do in eastern Crete: from turquoise beaches and impressive gorges to authentic villages 
and historic islands.

East Crete – largely located in the Lassithi region – is wilder, quieter and less touristy than 
the west of the island. Here you won't find mass tourism, but nature, space and the true Crete 
feeling.

In this overview, I've gathered 15 places you absolutely must see, neatly grouped by region.

Want to combine several places? Then I'd recommend renting a car in Crete – public transport in 
East Crete is limited. Be sure to read my article on 
[renting a car in Crete](/praktisch/auto-huren-op-kreta).

## Top 5 Attractions in East Crete

Short on time? These are the absolute highlights:

- Zakros Gorge – the most beautiful hike in East Crete
- Spinalonga – striking history on an island
- Itanos Beach – the best beach for those seeking peace and quiet
- Voulisma Beach (eastern part) – turquoise water without the crowds
- Ierapetra – the southernmost city in Europe

Below you'll find all 15 tips in detail.

## Agios Nikolaos & Elounda

This is the most accessible region of East Crete and an ideal home base.

### Agios Nikolaos

Agios Nikolaos is the lively heart of East Crete. The round Lake Voulismeni sits right in the 
middle of town, surrounded by restaurants and terraces.

Why visit?

- Cozy evening atmosphere
- Beautiful harbor
- Central location

Read my full guide on [Agios Nikolaos and the surrounding area](/gidsen/lassithi/agios-nikolaos).

### Elounda & Plaka

Elounda is known for its beautiful bay, while Plaka is smaller and more authentic. Boats to 
Spinalonga leave from both villages. Plaka is ideal for lunch with a sea view.

### Spinalonga

Spinalonga is one of the most striking historical sites on Crete. The island was first a Venetian 
fortress and later a leper colony.

Why visit?

- Impressive fortress walls
- Remarkable history
- Beautiful views over the bay

Read all about the island's history in my article on [Spinalonga](/gidsen/lassithi/spinalonga).

### Voulisma Beach

Voulisma Beach lies about 15 minutes from Agios Nikolaos and is known for its bright blue water.

Good to know:
The central section can get busy in summer. But those who walk to the eastern, lesser-known part 
often find more peace and just as beautiful turquoise water.

Why this beach is worth it:

- Easy to reach
- Ideal for families
- Stunning colors

Looking for more beach inspiration? Check out my overview of the 
[most beautiful beaches in Crete](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-stranden).

## Zakros & The Southeast

This is the wildest and most authentic part of East Crete.

### Zakros Gorge (Valley of the Dead)

The Zakros Gorge is one of the most beautiful hikes on Crete. The route starts in Ano Zakros and 
ends after about 8 kilometers at the sea.

What makes this gorge special:

- Impressive rock walls
- Relative tranquility
- A combination of nature and history

After the hike, you arrive in Kato Zakros.

### Kato Zakros

Kato Zakros is a small coastal village with a relaxed atmosphere.

Here you can:

- Relax on the beach
- Visit the Minoan palace
- Eat fresh fish by the sea

Discover all the details in my complete guide to [Kato Zakros](/gidsen/lassithi/kato-zakros). Want 
to discover more hikes? Check out my overview of 
[Crete's most beautiful gorges](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven).

## Vai, Itanos & Toplou

This part of East Crete feels almost exotic.

### Vai Palm Beach

Vai is unique in Europe thanks to its natural palm forest. The beach has a tropical feel and clear 
water.

Tip: walk to the viewpoint above the beach for the best panorama.

Read my full article on [Vai Beach](/gidsen/lassithi/vai-beach).

### Itanos Beach (My Personal Favorite)

A few minutes from Vai lies [Itanos Beach](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-stranden). This 
beach consists of several coves and is quieter than Vai.

Why many travelers consider this one of the most beautiful beaches in East Crete:

- Fewer crowds
- Untouched nature
- Great snorkeling opportunities

Looking for peace and quiet? Then Itanos often wins out over Vai.

### Toplou Monastery

The fortified [Toplou Monastery](/gidsen/cultuur/de-mooiste-kloosters) dates from the 15th century 
and played an important role in Cretan history.

Here you can:

- Visit a small museum
- Taste local wine
- Buy olive oil

Perfect to combine with Vai or Itanos.

## Ierapetra & The South Coast

Here you'll discover a warmer, drier side of Crete.

### Ierapetra

Ierapetra is the southernmost city in Europe and has an authentic atmosphere.

What you'll find here:

- Venetian fortress (Kales)
- Narrow streets in the old town
- Excellent seafood restaurants

Boats to Chrissi Island depart from the harbor.

### Makry Gialos

Makry Gialos means "long coast" and is ideal for families. Wide beaches and a quiet atmosphere 
make this a pleasant place to stay.

### Pefki Gorge

The [Pefki Gorge](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven) is less well-known and therefore 
quieter than Zakros. A shorter hike, suitable for beginners and families.

### Xerokampos

Xerokampos is a hidden gem for those seeking absolute peace and quiet.

Here you'll find:

- Remote beaches
- Few accommodations
- Untouched nature

## How Many Days Do You Need in East Crete?

For the main attractions in East Crete, you'll need at least 3 days. Want to hike several gorges 
and combine beaches? Then 5 to 7 days is ideal.

## Practical Tips for East Crete

- Rent a car for maximum freedom
- Fill up on gas whenever you can – distances are large
- Bring enough water for hikes
- Avoid August if you're seeking peace and quiet

## Frequently Asked Questions About East Crete

**What counts as East Crete?**
East Crete mainly covers the Lassithi region, including Agios Nikolaos, Elounda, Sitia, Ierapetra, 
Zakros and Vai.

**Is East Crete quieter than the west?**
Yes. The east is less touristy and feels more authentic.

**What's the most beautiful beach in East Crete?**
For peace and natural beauty, many travelers choose Itanos Beach. For turquoise water with a bit 
more comfort, the eastern part of Voulisma Beach is an excellent choice.

**Can you visit East Crete without a car?**
It's possible, but limited. For maximum flexibility, a rental car is strongly recommended.

**Is Agios Nikolaos still East Crete?**
Yes, Agios Nikolaos lies in the Lassithi region and is considered the gateway to East Crete.
