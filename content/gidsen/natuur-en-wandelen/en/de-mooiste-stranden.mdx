\*If you look up the most beautiful beaches on Crete, you'll get the same names everywhere: Balos, 
Elafonisi, Vai. Beautiful places, certainly, but often busy and commercial too. To me, they don't 
always show the quiet, natural Crete I love so much. That's why I've deliberately left the classics 
aside and I'm sharing the beaches I personally find worthwhile – places with space, simplicity and an 
atmosphere you won't find in every travel guide.\*

## Kommos Beach

Kommos, just past Matala, is a long stretch of beach on the south coast. It's a comfortable drive 
from both [Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion) and the 
[Messara Plain](/gidsen/heraklion/de-messaravlakte), making it ideal for anyone exploring those 
regions. The sand is darker and the waves can sometimes be fairly strong, but it feels spacious and 
calm, even when it's a bit busier.

For me, the charm isn't just the beach itself, but also up on the cliffs on the west side, where 
Taverna Magical View sits. That terrace gives you a view of the sea, the horizon and the long beach 
below. It's the kind of place where you naturally start talking more slowly, and where it's easy to 
spend a whole afternoon: simplicity, space and a beautiful view, all together.

## Triopetra Beach

Triopetra is one of Crete's quietest and most pleasant beaches, located in the southern part of the 
[Rethymnon](/gidsen/rethymnon/van-rethymno-tot-triopetra) region. Even in summer, there's plenty of 
space and the atmosphere stays relaxed and easygoing. The beach is actually made up of **two parts**. 
The well-known three rock formations split it into a right and left side. The **nicer side**, where 
_Taverna Apothiki_ is located, is my favorite: wide, quiet and with a natural feel.

Triopetra itself also has **two zones**: one with sunbeds and umbrellas, and an open stretch where 
you simply lay down your towel – a bit like the Belgian coast, but with a much more beautiful 
backdrop.

**Taverna Apothiki** is my go-to stop. Simple, friendly and with a sea view. You can grab a bite or 
just sit with a drink without anyone rushing you.

## Voulisma Beach

Voulisma is popular, and you can tell from the organized sections with bars and music. The beach 
lies a short distance from [Agios Nikolaos](/gidsen/lassithi/agios-nikolaos), making it an easy 
outing for many visitors. But what a lot of people don't know: the eastern side is only reachable on 
foot from the main road, and there it's a completely different story.

For me, that's exactly the part worth visiting: no rows of beach chairs, fewer people, and water 
that looks just as clear as in the photos. You immediately feel that it's calmer and more relaxed.

It's a great example of how a well-known beach can still hide quiet little corners.

## Kedrodasos Beach – An Alternative to Elafonisi

In the [Chania](/gidsen/chania/musea-van-chania) region, not far from Elafonisi, you'll find 
**Kedrodasos**, a beach that feels much more authentic to me. Elafonisi is often extremely crowded, 
and the "pink sand" tends to turn out less pink in reality than you'd expect.

Kedrodasos is different: dunes, low cedar trees, a wilder landscape, and no big bars or commercial 
setups. It takes a short walk to get there, but that's exactly what keeps the crowds away. It's a 
beach for anyone who wants to feel the natural, rugged side of Crete.

## Itanos Beach

All the way east, in the Lassithi region, above Vai, lies **Itanos** – a small series of beaches 
with clear water and a relaxed atmosphere. While [Vai](/gidsen/lassithi/vai-beach) can be very 
commercial and busy, Itanos feels much softer and calmer to me.

You can swim, wander among the archaeological remains, and above all enjoy yourself without feeling 
like you're standing in a busy tourist set piece.

## Agiofarago Beach

You reach Agiofarago on foot via a 
[gorge](/gidsen/natuur-en-wandelen/de-mooiste-kloven) – not a difficult hike, but one that keeps the 
crowds away. The high rock walls, the silence and the goats along the way already make it 
worthwhile before you even see the beach.

The bay itself is small, with pebbles and clear water. There are no facilities, so it's best to bring 
what you need. For me, this is a beach where the combination of gorge and sea is what makes it 
unique.

## Xerokambos Beach

Xerokambos lies in a quiet corner of East Crete. The sand is light, the water shallow and 
crystal-clear, and the atmosphere is simple. There are a few taverns and places to stay, but it 
remains a quiet area with plenty of open space. Ideal for spending an unplanned day at the beach.

## About Elafonisi and Vai

Since they're mentioned so often, one more note: **Elafonisi** and **Vai** look stunning in photos, 
but I find them very crowded and not very authentic. Elafonisi's pink sand is usually a lot less pink 
than it looks online, and the whole place often feels overcrowded.

For me, spots like **Kedrodasos** or **Itanos** offer a much more pleasant, natural experience.

## In Closing

The most beautiful beach isn't always the most famous one. Often, the quietest and most natural 
spots stay with you longer than the iconic names. For me, these are the beaches where you truly feel 
Crete: simplicity, nature and room to breathe. And sometimes that's all you need for a perfect day by 
the sea.
