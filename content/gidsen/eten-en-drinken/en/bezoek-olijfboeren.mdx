\*Anyone visiting Crete will, sooner or later, taste olive oil. Not just a flavoring, but the 
beating heart of the island. Here, the olive tree isn't just a tree, but a symbol of life — rooted 
in history, in the landscape, in the people themselves. All across the island you'll find family 
businesses proudly producing their own oil. A visit to a local olive farm is therefore far more than 
a tasting: it's a glimpse into Cretan craftsmanship.\*

## Lyrakis Family Olive Green Mill – Sisi (Lasithi)

A visit to the Lyrakis Family Olive Green Mill feels like coming home to people who put their heart 
and soul into their work. In summer it's quiet here, since the harvest doesn't happen until winter, 
but that's exactly when you notice the charm of the place: the scent of herbs in the little shop, 
the gleam of bottles on the shelves, and outside, that breathtaking view over the landscape.

You can taste several types of olive oil here, served with a piece of rusk (the hard Cretan 
biscuit). In summer, the room housing the machines is converted into a small shop selling not just 
olive oil, but also local honey, olives and herbs. Next to the shop is a cozy bar where you can sit 
down for a while with a coffee, a soft drink or a glass of wine. There's ample parking, and the view 
over the valley completes the picture — a wonderful stop on the way to or from Sissi.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Alonaki Sisiou, Sissi – 724 00, Lasithi.
**Visits & tour:** There's a free guided tour with olive oil tasting – 45 min.
**Price:** The basic tour is free.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://lyrakisfamily.gr/
</InfoBox>

## Spiridi Olive Oil Farm – Anissaras (Lassithi)

Not far from Hersonissos lies Spiridi Olive Oil Farm, a charming spot where visitors get 
acquainted with traditional production methods. Here, production still respects nature, with 
attention to sustainable cultivation and cold pressing. The tour is simple but authentic, and ends 
with a tasting that teaches you to recognize the difference between young and ripe olives. Their 
extra virgin olive oil with a peppery finish is especially worth trying.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** 2nd km Agios Nikolaos – Elounda, 721 00, Lasithi.
**Visits & tour:** Tour of the museum section and tasting – 60 min.
**Price:** €7 per person – €3 per child (6-12 yrs) – Free under age 6.
**Reservations:** No reservation needed – https://www.cretanoliveoilfarm.com/
</InfoBox>

## Paraschakis Olive Oil – Melidoni (Rethymnon)

In western Crete, near the village of Melidoni, lies the Paraschakis Olive Oil Factory. This 
family business spans generations and combines modern techniques with age-old tradition. During the 
tour, you see the whole process, from olive to bottled oil. A strong sense of family runs through 
it — you're often welcomed by one of the Paraschakis brothers himself, who proudly tell you about 
their work. The shop offers not just olive oil, but also soap and other olive-based products.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Epar.Od. Peramatos-Exantis, Exantis 740 52.
**Visits & tour:** Guided tour and tasting available.
**Price:** Free.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://paraschakis.gr/
</InfoBox>

## Kleanthi Olive Farm – Skalani (Heraklion)

Kleanthi Olive Farm is a small, organic farm just outside Heraklion. Here, everything revolves 
around quality over quantity. The owners are happy to explain how organic cultivation differs from 
conventional methods, and how Crete's terroir shapes the flavor of the oil. The tasting is 
small-scale and easygoing, with views over the olive groves.

Wine lovers can also stop by in the same village at 
[Scalarea Estate](/en/gidsen/eten-en-drinken/de-wijnhuizen-van-kreta), one of the local wineries — a 
perfect combination for a day of tasting local flavors.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Skalani, Heraklion 71500.
**Visits & tour:** Guided tour through the olive groves with olive oil tasting – 60 min.
**Price:** Via the official website, EUR 8 per person.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://www.kleanthievoo.com/
</InfoBox>

## Vassilakis Estate – Neapoli (Lassithi)

Vassilakis Estate is one of the best-known producers in East Crete, beloved for its refined oils, 
which regularly win international awards. The Vassilakis family places strong emphasis on 
transparency: visitors see exactly how the oil is pressed and stored, and receive an engaging 
explanation of purity and flavor profiles. The tasting in their modern visitor center is 
professional, yet remains warm and welcoming — typically Cretan.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Gerania, Neapoli, Lasithi, 72400.
**Visits & tour:** Friendly Olive Tour: olive oil mill & museum, with audio guide.
**Price:** Friendly Olive Tour is FREE – Paid tastings from EUR 20 per person.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://vassilakisestate.gr/tours/
</InfoBox>

## Toplou Monastery – Between Sitia and Palekastro (Lassithi)

And then there's [Toplou Monastery](/en/gidsen/cultuur/de-mooiste-kloosters), where olive oil takes 
on a spiritual dimension. The monks produce their own organic oil, sourced from centuries-old olive 
trees that adorn the rugged landscape of East Crete. A visit to Toplou feels like stepping back in 
time: stone walls, silvery leaves rustling in the wind, and the scent of incense mixed with 
freshly pressed oil. In the little shop by the monastery entrance, you can buy bottles that are not 
just a product, but a piece of history.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** 10 km east of Sitia, Toplou 723 00.
**Visits & tour:** Open daily from 9:00 to ~17:00. In the modern "Fabrica" tasting room, you can taste olive oil and wine.
**Price:** Tour and tasting from €15.50. The church and museum, EUR 4 per person.
**Reservations:** N/A
</InfoBox>
