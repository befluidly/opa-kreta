\*Crete has a wine tradition older than that of France or Italy. As far back as three thousand 
years ago, the Minoans made wine in clay jars, somewhere between olive trees and the sea. Today, 
families carry that centuries-old knowledge forward — with just as much love, and often on the 
same land.\*

## A Brief History of Cretan Wine

Archaeologists discovered in Vathypetro, close to Archanes (south of Heraklion), one of the 
oldest wine presses in the world — more than 3,500 years old. The [Minoans](/en/gidsen/heraklion/knossos) 
were already exporting their wine to Egypt and the Middle East. After centuries of invasions and 
occupations (Romans, Venetians, Ottomans), winemaking survived, often hidden away in villages and 
monasteries.

In the 20th century, a new chapter began: families like Lyrarakis and Douloufakis rediscovered 
native grape varieties that had nearly disappeared. This gave rise to the wine renaissance of 
[Heraklion](/en/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion), today the heart of the Cretan wine world.

## Why So Many Winemakers in the Heraklion Region?

The area around Heraklion brings together everything wine needs:

- Elevation differences (200–600 m) that create cool nights and crisp acidity in the wine.
- Fertile volcanic soils, rich in limestone and minerals.
- South-facing slopes with plenty of sunlight, sheltered from harsh sea winds.
- And above all: old villages like Archanes, Dafnes and Alagni, where wine has been a family 
tradition for centuries.

That's why most of Crete's modern wineries are found here — often family businesses that blend 
tradition and technology in harmony. A tour along these wineries isn't just a culinary outing, but 
a journey through the character of the island.

## Lyrarakis Winery – Alagni (Heraklion)

Among the rolling hills of Alagni lies Lyrarakis Winery, a pioneer in preserving Cretan grape 
varieties. The family started in the sixties with a mission: to bring forgotten varieties like 
Plyto and Vidiano back to life.

The tour takes you through sun-drenched vineyards with views of Psiloritis, followed by a tasting 
in the modern tasting room. The Plyto is refreshing and mineral-driven, the Kotsifali-Mandilari 
blend powerful and spicy.

## Manousakis Winery – Vatolakkos (Chania)

In the green hills of Vatolakkos, overlooking the White Mountains, lies Manousakis Winery — an 
oasis of calm where nature and tradition meet. The family works entirely organically, with grapes 
like Syrah and Roussanne, aging the wine in French oak barrels.

On the terrace, surrounded by cypress trees and lavender, you taste their wines paired with local 
bites. The Nostos Assyrtiko has a briny freshness, while the Syrah is full-bodied, soft and spicy — 
a glass that smells like summer evenings.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Vatolakkos 73005, Chania.
**Visits & tour:** Tours are free and offered daily at 12pm, 2pm, 4pm and 6pm.
**Price:** Taste 5 wines, accompanied by rusk, olive oil and sea salt – EUR 15 per person
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://www.manousakiswinery.com/
</InfoBox>

## Douloufakis Winery – Dafnes (Heraklion)

In Dafnes, where the soil turns red with iron and sun, you'll find Douloufakis Winery — a family 
business combining modern techniques with a centuries-old wine soul. The Liatiko, a local grape 
that's grown here since antiquity, is their pride and joy.

Visitors can choose between the "Yamas" tour (six wines, about 80 minutes) or the more extensive 
"Arismari" tour with bites in the vineyard. The welcome is warm and easygoing: a smile, a glass, a 
story.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Dafnes 70011, Heraklion.
**Visits & tour:** Tour included when booking a tasting.
**Price:** Yamas €22 p.p. (80 min.) · Arismari €38 p.p. (2 hours)
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://douloufakis.wine/
</InfoBox>

## Karavitakis Winery – Pontikiana (Chania)

Karavitakis Winery is tucked away among olive groves in the village of Pontikiana. Here you taste 
the charm of family life: simplicity, know-how and pride.

The tour takes you past old wine barrels and new ideas. The Vidiano, Malvasia and Syrah wines show 
the versatility of Crete: powerful yet elegant. The atmosphere is calm, and the view stretches out 
over a sea of vines.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Pontikiana, Chania.
**Visits & tour:** Tour included when booking a tasting – 60 min.
**Price:** Taste 5 different wines or more, from EUR 15 p.p.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://www.karavitakiswines.com/
</InfoBox>

## Scalarea Estate – Skalani (Heraklion)

On the outskirts of Heraklion lies the stylish Scalarea Estate, where modern wine architecture and 
Cretan earth come together. The vineyards sit on cool slopes with sea views, ideal for fresh, 
aromatic wines.

The Vidiano is one of their flagship wines: subtly floral with notes of peach and citrus peel. In 
the tasting room, you can choose between short or extended tastings, paired with matching cheeses.

Olive oil lovers can also stop by in the same village at 
[Kleanthi Olive Farm](/en/gidsen/eten-en-drinken/bezoek-olijfboeren), one of the local producers of 
olive oil in this area — a perfect combination for a day of tasting local flavors.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Skalani 70100, Heraklion.
**Visits & tour:** Tours and tastings by appointment.
**Price:** Taste 4 different wines or more, from EUR 25 p.p.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://scalareaestate.gr/
</InfoBox>

## Silva Daskalaki Winery – Siva (Heraklion)

Just outside [Heraklion](/en/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion), in the village of Siva, the 
Daskalaki family runs a vineyard where passion and sustainability go hand in hand. They work fully 
organically and biodynamically — the wine lives along with the seasons.

The Enstikto White is light and fragrant, while the Emphasis Vidiano embodies elegance in a glass. 
The tasting takes place on a sun-drenched terrace overlooking the hills.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Siva 70011, Heraklion.
**Visits & tour:** There are 6 different guided tours with tasting.
**Price:** Tasting of 6 different wines, accompanied by rusk and Cretan cheeses – EUR 19 p.p.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://www.silvawines.gr/
</InfoBox>

## Diamantakis Winery – Kato Asites (Heraklion)

Diamantakis Winery lies in Kato Asites, at the foot of Mount Psiloritis. The view alone is worth 
the detour. The family produces elegant wines focused on Vidiano and Mandilaria, grapes that bring 
the volcanic terroir to life.

Their Petali Vidiano is one of Crete's finest white wines: soft, complex and mineral-driven. The 
welcome is warm and personal, with clear explanations of production and aging.

<InfoBox>
**Address & contact:** Kato Asites 70013, Heraklion.
**Visits & tour:** Choice of 2 tours and tastings by appointment.
**Price:** Tour and tasting of 4 wines €13 p.p.
**Reservations:** Book via the website – https://www.diamantakiswines.gr/
</InfoBox>

## Klados Winery – Panormos (Rethymnon)

On the north coast, just outside the charming town of Panormos, lies Klados Winery, a small but 
refined family business. Under the pergola, you taste local wines with a sea view — simplicity at 
its best.

The Vidiano White is lively and aromatic; the Liatiko Red soft and spicy. The Klados family proudly 
tells the story of how, since 1997, they've shaped their dream: making quality wine with respect 
for nature.
