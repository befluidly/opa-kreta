\*Anyone wanting to visit the [Palace of Knossos](/gidsen/heraklion/knossos) usually has one 
practical question first: how much does a ticket cost, and when is the best time to go to 
avoid the crowds? Below you'll find a clear and up-to-date overview for 2026. This guide 
helps you prepare well for your visit to Knossos and avoid waiting times.\*

## What Does a Ticket for Knossos Cost in 2026?

The standard ticket price for the Palace of Knossos in 2026 is:

- €20 for adults
- €10 for those 65 and older
- Free up to age 25 (EU residents)
- Free up to age 18 (Non-EU residents)

Children, EU youth and certain categories of visitors may be entitled to free or reduced 
admission. The exact conditions sometimes vary from year to year, so always check shortly 
before your visit.

During the summer months, waiting times at the ticket office can build up, especially 
between 10am and 2pm.

## Is There Still a Combined Ticket for Knossos?

No. The former combined ticket for the Palace of Knossos and the 
[Archaeological Museum of Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion/musea-van-heraklion) no longer exists. 
Visitors now buy separate tickets for both locations.

Those wanting to visit both Knossos and the museum are best planning this on the same day or 
on two consecutive days. The museum also provides extra context for what you see at the 
archaeological site, so the combination is still very worthwhile — even without a combined ticket.

Because ticket rules in Greece sometimes change, it's wise to check the official information 
shortly before your visit.

## When Is Knossos Free to Visit?

Admission to archaeological sites and museums under the Greek Ministry of Culture — including 
the Palace of Knossos — is completely free on certain days.

In 2026, free admission applies on:

- March 6 ([Melina Mercouri](/tips/muziek/melina-merkouri) Memorial Day)
- April 18 (International Monuments Day)
- May 18 (International Museum Day)
- The last weekend of September (European Heritage Days)
- October 28 (Greek National Holiday)
- The first and third Sunday of each month from November 1 through March 31

On these days, Knossos is fully free to enter, but expect extra crowds at the entrance.

## Buy Tickets Online or On-Site?

### Buying Tickets On-Site

Advantages:

- No advance planning needed
- Flexible

Disadvantages:

- Queues in July and August
- Crowds around cruise ship arrivals
- Less control over your timing

### Booking Tickets Online

Advantages:

- Faster entry
- Less stress on busy days
- Peace of mind during high season

Disadvantages:

- Sometimes a small service fee
- Less flexible for last-minute changes
- During high season (June – September), booking online is usually the safest choice.

## When Is Knossos Busiest?

Knossos is one of Crete's most popular attractions. Crowd levels vary a lot by time period.

Busiest moments:

- Between 10am and 2pm
- July & August
- Days when cruise ships dock in Heraklion
- Weekend days in summer

Quieter moments:

- Right after opening
- Late afternoon
- April, May and October

## What's the Best Time to Visit Knossos?

In summer, it can get very hot at the site. There's little natural shade, so a midday visit 
can be less pleasant.

The best time is:

- Early in the morning (before 9:30am)
- Or later in the afternoon (after 4pm)

In spring and fall, the temperature is more comfortable and you can usually get in without 
long waits. If you're traveling in July or August, it's best to plan your visit outside peak hours.

## How Do You Avoid Queues?

Want to minimize waiting?

- Go early or late in the day
- Avoid cruise ship days
- Buy your ticket in advance during high season
- Visit Knossos on a weekday instead of the weekend

A bit of planning ahead really makes a difference.

## Practical Tips for Your Visit

- Bring enough water (especially in summer)
- Wear a hat or cap
- Choose comfortable shoes
- Set aside at least 1.5 to 2 hours for your visit

Knossos is about a 15–20 minute drive from Heraklion, making it easy to combine with a visit 
to the city or the museum.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Knossos Tickets

### Do You Need to Book Knossos in Advance?

Usually not necessary in low season. In July and August, it's strongly recommended, especially 
if you're going around midday.

### How Long Is Your Ticket Valid?

Your ticket is valid for one visit during that day's opening hours.

### Can You Pay by Card On-Site?

Yes, usually. Still, carrying some cash is never a bad idea.

### Is Knossos Open Every Day?

Knossos is open nearly year-round, but opening hours may vary on holidays or during low season.
