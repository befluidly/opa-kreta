## How Long Do You Need at Knossos? With Kids, a Guide, or Without (2026)

\*Anyone visiting the [Palace of Knossos](/gidsen/heraklion/knossos) often wonders how much time to 
set aside. Is it suitable for kids? Do you need a guide? How do you get there from Heraklion? 
Below you'll find a complete and practical overview for 2026.\*

### How Long Does a Visit to Knossos Take?

Most visitors spend 1.5 to 2 hours at the archaeological site of Knossos.

That depends on:

- Your interest in history
- Whether you use a guide or audio guide
- How crowded it is
- The temperature
- Whether you're traveling with kids

#### Quick Visit (± 1 hour)

Those who mainly want to see the key parts — such as the Central Court, the Throne Room and the 
iconic red columns — can be done in about an hour.

#### Extended Visit (± 2 to 2.5 hours)

With a guide or audio guide, you'll take more time. You'll get explanations about the Minoan 
civilization, the myths of King Minos and the Minotaur, and the reconstructions visible today.

Anyone genuinely interested in history should plan for two hours.

### Is Knossos Suitable for Visiting with Kids?

Yes, but with a few things to keep in mind.

#### What Works Well with Kids?

- The story of the Minotaur sparks the imagination
- It's an open site where kids can move around freely
- The site isn't extremely large

#### What Should You Keep in Mind?

- Little shade in summer
- Uneven stone ground
- No play areas
- Heat can be tiring quickly

With young children, it's best to plan your visit early in the morning. Bring water, sun protection 
and sturdy shoes. For teens interested in mythology, Knossos is often a genuinely engaging experience.

#### With a Stroller?

Because of the uneven ground, a stroller isn't ideal. A baby carrier is more practical.

### Do You Need a Guide at Knossos?

Knossos is impressive, but without explanation you'll mostly see ruins.

#### Without a Guide

Advantages:

- Flexible pace
- Cheaper
- You follow your own route

Disadvantages:

- Less context
- Harder to put everything into perspective

#### With a Guide or Audio Guide

Advantages:

- You understand better what you're seeing
- Historical background and stories
- More efficient route

For those with limited time or who really want to understand how the 
[Minoan civilization](/gidsen/heraklion/knossos) lived, a guide is highly recommended.

### Getting There from Heraklion

Knossos is located about 5 kilometers from the center of [Heraklion](/gidsen/heraklion/bezoek-heraklion).

#### By Car

- 15 to 20 minutes' [drive](/praktisch/auto-huren-op-kreta)
- Parking nearby (paid during high season)

#### By Bus

- Regular connection from Heraklion
- Ride takes about 20 minutes
- Bus 2 "Knossos" from the main station at the harbor in Heraklion

That makes it easy to combine Knossos with the [Archaeological Museum](/gidsen/heraklion/musea-van-heraklion) 
or a walk through the city.

### Is Knossos Wheelchair Accessible?

There is access for people with disabilities from the palace entrance to the Central Court.

Important to know:

- Not all parts of the site are fully accessible
- The ground is uneven
- It remains a historical archaeological site

Those with specific needs should check for the most current accessibility information beforehand.

### Are There Places to Eat and Drink?

At and around the site you'll find:

- A small kiosk or café near the entrance
- Souvenir shops
- Taverns and restaurants in the immediate area

Within the archaeological zone itself, facilities are limited. Especially in summer, bringing 
enough water is important.

### Opening Hours of Knossos (2026)

Opening hours vary by season.

#### General

- Summer period: usually around 08:00 – 20:00 (last entry 19:45)
- Winter period: shorter hours 08:00 - 18:00 (last entry 17:45)

Opening hours may change slightly each year. Always check the official website of the Ministry 
of Culture shortly before your visit.

### Free Admission Days

On certain days, admission to Knossos is completely free.

Free admission applies on:

- March 6 (Melina Mercouri Memorial Day)
- April 18 (International Monuments Day)
- May 18 (International Museum Day)
- The last weekend of September (European Heritage Days)
- October 28 (Greek National Holiday)

On these days, it can be significantly busier than usual.

### Closed or Adjusted Hours

On certain holidays, adjusted hours apply:

- Orthodox Good Friday: 12:00 – 17:00
- Orthodox Holy Saturday: 08:00 – 16:00
- Orthodox Easter Sunday: closed
- May 1 (Labor Day): closed
- October 28: 08:00 – 18:00 (free admission)

Holidays and opening hours may change from year to year.

### When Should You Combine Knossos with the Museum?

Many visitors combine Knossos with the Archaeological Museum of Heraklion.

A logical order is:

- Visit Knossos first
- Then the museum for the original finds

That way, you get a more complete picture of the Minoan world.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is 1 Hour Enough for Knossos?

For a brief impression, yes. For a full visit, 1.5 to 2 hours is more realistic.

#### Is Knossos Hard to Walk Around?

The site isn't extremely large, but the ground is uneven. Comfortable shoes are important.

#### Is It Suitable with a Stroller?

Difficult. A baby carrier is more practical.
